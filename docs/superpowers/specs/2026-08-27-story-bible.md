# Valheim: The Saga — story bible

Written 2026-08-27, at `0.221.12-run.alpha74`, answering the owner's question:
*"Why do the trees need the light? What's our story?"*

## The premise

**Nothing in this world can make its own light.** Valheim is the tenth world —
where Odin sends the dead, the defeated, the put-away. Everything here is
*after* its life. Dead things do not generate light; they can only take it.

**The deer are the exception.** Eikthyr's herd carries original light — sparks
from before this world was a prison. The herd banks it by day, which is why
the hunt is nocturnal: only in the dark can a light rise at all.

**The Elder is a hunger wearing a tree.** Dead wood that learned to stay
standing by drinking what the deer carry. The greydwarves are splinters of
him — animated deadwood that goes dark and still unless fed. They swarm every
carcass out of starvation, not malice. The Gatherer was the biggest, oldest
splinter, fat enough to have a name.

**The player arrives alive** — the first new light in an age. That is why the
forest notices the axe, why the Gatherer follows the hunt, why the ravens have
started landing. **Odin did not send you to slay gods; he sent you to find out
where the light is going.** The ravens are his audit. The gods keep being in
the way of the answer.

## Each act is a failed answer to the same shortage

| Act | Title | Their answer |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Stolen Light | (the theft itself — the herd farmed) |
| II | Where the Light Goes | **Steal it.** The Elder farms the herd |
| III | Nothing Stays Buried | **Never let go.** The swamp hoards its dead's light — that is why nothing stays down, and Bonemass is everything that never let go |
| IV | The White Silence | **It is already gone.** The mountains froze when theirs ran out. Moder guards eggs: light that has not woken |
| V | The Golden Ruin | **Industrialise it.** Yagluth's people harvested at scale; the ruin is what is left when the harvest runs dry |
| VI | A Light to Carry | **Carry it honestly.** The dvergr keep wisps in lanterns — borrowed and returned. The only ones who got it right |
| VII | The Last Light | **Let it burn.** Where light goes to end |

## Rules this imposes on content

- Light rises only in the dark, everywhere, always. The night gate is lore,
  not a difficulty knob.
- The forest's creatures converge on light out of hunger; they never destroy
  it, they carry it. Nothing that takes a light should read as vandalism.
- The player's own light is never mechanised. It is why things notice them,
  not a resource with a number.
- Ravens appear at moments Odin would want witnessed. They never help.
- The wisp item taken from a race is the same species of thing the dvergr
  lantern carries in Act VI — the arc's payoff is that the player has been
  collecting honestly since Act I.
- Killing a god settles that god's hoard, but never solves the shortage. The
  shortage is the saga; the answer is Act VII's.

## Already true in play (retro-fitted by accident, kept on purpose)

Starred deer (light concentrating), the Herald (the herd's guardian), lightning
at deer kills (Eikthyr's grief), packs at carcasses (starving splinters), the
Gatherer growing on lost lights (hunger rewarded), strays (lights not yet
caught), the Act II epigraph ("The forest has been fed for years").
