## The story

The myth is `docs/THE-SAGA.md` (about 16,000 words); the step-by-step telling is
`docs/SAGA-WALKTHROUGH.md`; the rules are in the story bible, `specs/2026-08-27-story-bible.md`.

Nothing in the tenth world, Odin's store-room for the put-away, makes its own light, except Eikthyr's
deer. The sea puts a living person ashore, and Odin's ravens watch where the light goes.

| Act | Title | In short |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Stolen Light | the light race, the Gatherer, Thor's bow at the Storm-Anvil |
| II | Where the Light Goes | the barrow-keeper, the couriers, Haldor |
| III | Nothing Stays Buried | the Bog Witch, the drowned one, the Stormward made heavier |
| IV | The White Silence | the frozen one, woken by fire and paid with a dragon egg; Hildir |
| V | The Golden Ruin | the harvester, a meal from the field, the Sealed Tower, a feast at home |

Act VIII (the Deep North) has the game's real names since 2026-10-06, but its story is a stand-in.
Whether it is an eighth act or the epilogue the 2026-09-20 decision #3 asked for is still the owner's
call.

## The boats

The owner's aim: sailing to a god in another biome should be easy.

- **Ship fittings** (`ShipFittings`, `Shipwright`). Gold at any helm (Shift+E) buys Sail I–III (+15/30/50%
  under sail and oars), Hull I–III, and Fire-tar from Act VII. They follow the captain: every loaded ship
  wears them, and they are restored when the run ends.
- **The god's wind** (`SagaWind`, `SagaWinds`). With the act's god pinned on the map, a ship whose prow is
  within 40° of the altar gets the game's own Moder wind.
- **The Wind-horn.** A fourth fitting (200 coins): `↑` at sea gives two minutes of wind at your back,
  then ten minutes to recover.

What the test should bring back: whether the fitting prices fit the gold actually found, whether the
40° cone feels right, and whether the horn's 2 and 10 minutes do.

## New ideas, and where each stands

### Making better use of what the game ships

The **catalogue** is in `docs/catalog/` (2026-10-06): 162 creatures, 689 pieces, 1,519 items, 216
locations, and all 6,058 English game texts. `saga-usage.md` beside it rates how deeply the saga uses
each notable thing.

The biggest gaps:

- **Never used:** Munin (on purpose), the Valkyrie, and Odin as a figure. His cloaked model (`odin`) is
  unused.
- **One act only:** Haldor (Act II) and the Bog Witch (Act III). Hildir appears only in Acts IV–V, her
  first chest is unused, and she is never met earlier.
- **The Obliterator** is the heart of Act I, and after that only mends shields. It accepts every trophy as
  an input.
- **Boss items:** Yagluth's, the Queen's and Fader's are absent.
- **The Deep North's whole ending sequence is unused.** You break the Black Ice core, open the gate with
  Malicious Blood, fight Kall Fimbulbringer ("the Shackled One") in three forms, and finish on "You are
  worthy" at a stone platform.
- **Story material the game already has:**
  - Hervor's journal: a previous champion's road.
  - The frozen ships of earlier settlers.
  - Captives in Mörkhalla.
  - A hidden White Deer, with its own trophy.
  - The traders' companions: Halstein, Blåbär and Hallon, Kvastur.
  - Hildir's three quest bosses.

Status: being designed (brainstorming). The owner wants each thing to get a mix of a part in the
story, a use in play, and a big moment. Design now, build after the home test of `...06`, then build in
small slices that get played and adjusted.

### Heroes against Fader

The owner's long-standing wish: call down heroes to help against a boss like Fader ("rebuilding
Valheim"). The game ships the cast:

- **The Seven Aspects** (`Aspect_Eikthyr` … `Aspect_SeekerQueen`): creatures, echoes of the earlier
  bosses, which the Frozen King summons in his second form.
- **Fallen Warriors** at the Ancestral Memorial: named Norse warriors, with duel lines like "You are
  worthy."
- **`Skeleton_Friendly`** and the hidden spirit-caller creatures.
- **Each boss speaks after death** (`deadspeak_*`), which fits the bible's "gods speak only once defeated".
- **`Valkyrie_End`**, the vanilla "Journey to Valhalla".

The myth's premise fits too: the tenth world holds "the dead who did not die well enough for his hall".

A possible shape: at Fader you call down whoever you earned, and in the Deep North the Frozen King turns
them against you.

**What "rebuilding Valheim" means (owner, 2026-10-06):** "since this is a building game, if we could make
some quests around rebuilding Valheim (a section for each act) until the Ashlands act, where we complete
the build and can call down the gods we killed to help us against Fader."

This joins two older ideas into one spine. GitHub issue #17 ("build the halls of Valhalla, with
guidance") asks for a hall the player builds, guided and judged. Decision #4 (2026-09-20) gives empty high
seats by default, and a god who comes only if earned.

**Decided so far (2026-10-06, owner's answers):**

- **Shape: the hall is the spine.** A HALL questline runs through every act, and the underused cast
  attaches to it. The rest of the catalogue stays on a backlog, pulled in act by act.
- **Where: at home,** wherever the player lives. Each act adds a section around the first long fire.
  Act I's HEARTH questline becomes its first section, and Act V's feast at home moves into it.
- **Guidance, not blueprints** (#17). The saga says what a section needs; the player decides how it looks.
- **Each god's place** is its trophy on an item stand beside a seat, raised in that god's own act. A place
  not raised stays empty, that god does not come, and Fader stays winnable alone.
- **The call: only at Fader.** Bosses II–VI stay as hard as they are.
- **One horn, all at once.** One blow during the fight brings down every seated god for about a minute.
  They come as the game's own Aspects (`Aspect_Eikthyr` … `Aspect_SeekerQueen`), on the player's side.

**Designed and specced:** [`specs/2026-10-06-the-hall-design.md`](specs/2026-10-06-the-hall-design.md),
awaiting the owner's review. In short:

- **Sections by act:** I the long fire; II the frame; III iron and light (the Bog Witch's candle wicks);
  IV stone; V the roof and the feast (Hildir's fireworks); VI black marble; VII the finished hall with
  the Bone Throne as the player's own seat.
- **A god's place** is his trophy on an item stand beside a seat, raised in the act after his death,
  because the trophy only exists then. A place raised later, before Fader, still counts.
- **The hall replaces the sacrificial stones** (owner's twist): raising a god's place grants his power,
  merged with the existing "Claim X's power" step, and powers are switched at home with Shift+E. The
  vanilla stones still work, but raise no place.
- **When the hall is finished:** Odin's cloaked figure comes, looks and goes, which is the verdict. The
  horn is forged at the Storm-Anvil from Eikthyr's hard antler and flametal.
- **At Fader,** `↓` blows the horn once per attempt. Every placed god's Aspect fights beside you for about
  a minute, speaking his own after-death line.
- **Built after the home test, one slice at a time.** Slice 0 is a dev key testing whether an Aspect can
  be turned to the player's side, with three fallbacks if not.

### Valheim Smith: a platform for other people's mods

A new repo for Valheim players with an AI coding tool who have never modded. They clone it, ask the AI to
set it up, and see "My mod v0.1" on the main menu.

- A general mod kit with an optional story layer, on BepInEx and Harmony.
- One `smith` command-line tool and a set of AI skills.
- Windows, Mac and Steam Deck.
- It starts empty, with examples; saga mode is rebuilt on top of it later.

Status (2026-10-06): the spec is approved, and the plan for milestones 0–1 is written. The repo is **private on
GitHub**: `zrrbite/valheim-smith`. Milestone 1's Tasks 5–6 (`mod.json` and its tests; `smith` finding Steam, the
game and its version) are built and reviewed on branch `milestone-1-skeleton`. Next is milestone 0, which needs the
owner at the Mac: does BepInEx load, and does a Harmony patch take effect, on Apple Silicon?
